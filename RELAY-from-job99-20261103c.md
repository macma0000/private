DOCTRINE
1. Taking in and noticing: "What has been taken in stays out of view until a person wants something in it." 2. His edit "the easing [adds to the panic holding] the turning away in place" does not stand. Panic happens once, at the burial. Keep "the easing is what holds the turning away in place", or name the returning anxiety instead of panic.
