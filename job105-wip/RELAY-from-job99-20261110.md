The summit, and what counterfeits it
At the top there is nothing [further to reach, and a person does not stay at the top either]. [Even at the top, every thought’s detached phase ends at the arising, which takes the thought back into engagement, and the next thought does the same.] So [the clarity at the top needs to be renewed] rather than held.
It could not be held in any case, and the reason is the same one that runs the whole book. A state maintained is maintained by a habit of maintaining it, and that habit would need its own maintaining, and there is no floor to that. Habit is what carries a fixation and habit is what carries an attainment; it does not become a different faculty at the top of the ladder.
There is also something that looks like the summit and is not. [Clarity is detachment without distraction. Detachment in one realm can look, from outside, like clarity in both.]
[Calm in one realm can come without any training. A person whose fixation in a realm is detached gives the detached phase of thoughts about that realm more attention than an enthusiastic or cautious person does. Training to abide in the detached phase does the same on purpose. While nothing is disturbing a person detached in a realm, nothing in that realm pulls at the person, and nothing shows whether the person would reach for a distraction if something did. Observers can take the calm for clarity in both realms.]
Every thought passes through the detached phase, whatever a person’s fixation. A person can train themselves to abide in the detached phase, giving the detached phase more attention than their fixation would give it. The training can be done for thoughts about one realm and not the other. No orientation is trained, and the person’s type does not change.
Observers see the marks of the training in one realm and take the person to be like that throughout. What observers recognize as holiness is mostly these marks — austerity, fearlessness, unconcern with comfort and pain, a steadiness nothing seems to shake. The marks are real, and the mistake is the observers’. In every case the attainment is real. The counterfeit summit is a genuine, hard-won attainment in one realm, wearing the visible marks of the whole one, with the person’s other realm running entirely unchecked behind the attainment. What can differ is whether the person makes use of how others read the attainment.
Take a person trained to work on a bleeding body, or to keep moving under fire. At such a moment the person abides in the detached phase of thoughts about physical matters, and does not flinch or look away. The training is real, and so is the steadiness. The person reaches for the training when fear would get in the way, and uses the training as a tool at those moments. Nothing about the person’s type has changed.
Pain, hardship and danger no longer move the person, and the person shows no fear of harm or of death. Observers take that fearlessness as a sign of clarity about the inner life as well. The training has not touched the person’s psychic realm. The person still has the wants they had before about psychic matters, such as wanting to be admired or to be seen as righteous. The person may then present the fearlessness as the summit on purpose, because being taken for someone at the summit brings admiration and followers. Presenting the fearlessness that way is a fraud.
Take a person trained to sit with someone in the worst hour of their life and stay level, hearing what is said without being pulled into it. At such a moment the person abides in the detached phase of thoughts about psychic matters. Again the training is real, and again it is a tool used at moments. Nothing about the person’s type has changed here either.
[Such a person] is no longer moved by praise, blame or other people’s feelings. The person still wants money and comfort as before. The steadiness lets the person pursue those wants among other people without being moved by what those people feel. Others take the steadiness for the summit. Presenting the steadiness that way on purpose is a fraud as well. Nothing in the person checks what the attainment is used for.
Conscience is caution in the psychic realm: understanding the disadvantages of hurting others. For a person cautious in the psychic realm, conscience is their own fixation at work. While the person abides in the detached phase of thoughts about psychic matters, conscience is not raised. When the person stops abiding, conscience is there as before. A person enthusiastic in the psychic realm examines little of what their wants would do to others, so whatever conscience the person has was learned as a rule. A person who works for long without the rule may stop invoking the rule, and then stop having it. If that happens, the long working without the rule is the cause, and the training is not.
It would be reasonable to expect that training to abide in the detached phase weakens the person’s fixation in the trained realm, when the fixation is enthusiastic or cautious. An enthusiastic fixation and a cautious fixation are both ways of engaging with a realm, and abiding in the detached phase works against engaging.
