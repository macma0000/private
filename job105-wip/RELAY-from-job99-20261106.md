Job99 also pointed out a mismatch that the 11-04 change created. Chapter 7 already carries both (“The less caught a person is in the orientation they hold, the more of what the step requires they can bear.”), and so does Part 2’s opening. So only Chapter 10 changes. [A], 15:18: “yes.”
