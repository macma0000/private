THE RULE

* “The caution end” and “the enthusiasm end” name only the two ends of the detached phase.
* A person’s lean is always “leaning toward caution” or “leaning toward enthusiasm”. Never “a person near the caution end” or “a person near the enthusiasm end”.
* Write “the caution end of the detached phase” or “the enthusiasm end of the detached phase” in full at least once in every paragraph that uses either end. This replaces the rule of 09-13 (the phase named at the first mention in each section).
